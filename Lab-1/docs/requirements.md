# Requirements Specification – EV Charging Station Reservation System

## 1. Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FR-001** | Functional | The system shall match the driver's registered vehicle connector type (CCS, CHAdeMO, Type 2) with available station chargers and allow the driver to reserve a guaranteed 30-minute charging window on a compatible socket. | High | **Pass:** Only compatible, free sockets are shown; a confirmed reservation blocks that socket for the 30-minute window. **Fail:** An incompatible plug type is displayed, or two vehicles are booked on the same socket for overlapping times. | Core purpose of the system; prevents wasted trips to stations with incompatible plugs and removes queueing uncertainty. |
| **FR-002** | Functional | The system shall let the driver search for charging stations by current location or address within a chosen radius, filter by connector type, power rating (kW) and live availability, and provide navigation to the selected station. | High | **Pass:** Results within the radius are listed nearest-first with live socket status refreshed at least every 30 s; selecting a station opens turn-by-turn navigation. **Fail:** A station or socket under maintenance is shown as available. | The "navigation portal" half of the problem statement; drivers must discover suitable stations before they can reserve. |
| **FR-003** | Functional | The system shall calculate and display the dynamic power rate (price per kWh and estimated session cost) before the driver confirms a reservation, based on time of day, station demand and charger power rating, and shall lock the quoted rate for that reservation. | High | **Pass:** The quoted rate and estimate are shown before confirmation, and the rate billed equals the rate quoted. **Fail:** The rate changes after confirmation, or the booking can be confirmed without a rate being shown. | Dynamic pricing is explicitly required; price transparency builds trust and shifts load to off-peak periods. |
| **FR-004** | Functional | The system shall allow the driver to view, modify or cancel an upcoming reservation, applying the operator's refund policy (full refund when cancelled at least 30 minutes before the slot start), and shall return the freed slot to the available pool. | Medium | **Pass:** A cancelled slot is bookable by other drivers within 10 s and the refund matches the configured policy. **Fail:** A cancelled slot remains blocked, or a refund is calculated incorrectly. | Plans change; without cancellation, sockets sit idle and drivers lose money, reducing utilisation and satisfaction. |
| **FR-005** | Functional | The system shall allow the Station Operator to add and edit chargers, set a charger status (available / out of service), configure pricing rules and no-show penalty amounts, and view utilisation reports; an out-of-service charger shall not be bookable, and drivers with existing reservations on it shall be notified and offered rebooking or a refund. | Medium | **Pass:** A charger marked out of service disappears from search results immediately and affected drivers are notified within 1 minute. **Fail:** A driver can still reserve an out-of-service charger. | Operators need control over their assets and tariffs; this is the second stakeholder named in the problem statement. |

## 2. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NFR-001** | Performance & Security | The system shall automatically release a reserved socket and apply a no-show penalty if the driver has not plugged in within 10 minutes of slot start; release shall occur within 30 seconds of the deadline, and slot allocation shall be atomic so that no socket is double-booked under up to 5,000 concurrent users. | High | **Pass:** Benchmarking under a simulated peak load of 5,000 concurrent users shows 100 % of no-show sockets released within 30 s of the 10-minute mark, zero double bookings, and the penalty charged only to the driver's tokenised payment method. **Fail:** A socket stays locked beyond 10 min 30 s, or any double booking occurs. | Idle locked sockets waste scarce capacity and block other drivers; atomic locking protects the core booking guarantee. |
| **NFR-002** | Availability & Security | The system shall be available at least 99.9 % per calendar month, respond to station search requests within 3 seconds for 95 % of requests, encrypt all data in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher, and never store raw card data (payments tokenised through a PCI-DSS-compliant gateway). | High | **Pass:** Monitoring shows ≥ 99.9 % monthly uptime and p95 search latency ≤ 3 s; a security scan confirms TLS ≥ 1.2 and no card numbers in the database or logs. **Fail:** Uptime below target, p95 latency above 3 s, or any plaintext card data found. | Drivers rely on the service when they are on the road with low battery; payments and personal data must be protected. |
